# NP-09 — TRANSFORMATION TIERS: The Power-Ceiling Raisers

**Project Northpaw design vault · Doc 9/13**
Related: [[NP_05_TENSION_HISSATSU]] · [[NP_07_HOUSES]] · [[NP_08_STABLE]] · [[NP_12_NARRATIVE]]

One new ceiling per arc, stacked on everything before it, mirroring shonen escalation exactly. Every transformation costs the entire team Tension bar — the ultimate always empties the tank, so every ultimate is a commitment the whole team paid for. Presentation is Xenoverse-grade and played dead straight (Doctrine D1). This doc gives full rules, numbers, presentation scripts, counterplay, and the AI's usage logic for all five tiers.

---

## 1. SHARED RULES (all tiers)

- **Cost:** 100 team Tension (FULL RELAY discount: 75, [[NP_05_TENSION_HISSATSU]] §6). Meter empties on cast, always.
- **Cast:** SURGE motion (LB + both sticks click). Uninterruptible once the cinematic starts (the one uncounterable class in the game — priced accordingly).
- **One transformation per fighter per dual.** A second fighter may transform in a later segment if the team refills — multi-transform duals are late-game earned spectacle.
- **Duration-based, never permanent.** When it ends, a **Comedown**: 10 s at 85% stats (the price of glory; also the enemy's answer window — drama both ways).
- **AI usage:** enemy transforms are *authored per boss* (desperation blocks, [[NP_04_OPPONENT]] §2) — never systemic RNG. The player must be able to feel a boss transform *coming* narratively.

## 2. TIER 1 — HISSATSU (Arc 1 baseline)

Fully specified in [[NP_05_TENSION_HISSATSU]]. Listed here as the ladder's first rung: named technique → the fiction that technique itself can be *elevated*. The CRBF's "elevated arts" charter line ([[NP_01_SPORT]] §5) is the lore seed every later tier grows from.

## 3. TIER 2 — IN THE ZONE (Arc 1 finale unlock)

The personal-mastery tier: no aura creatures, no ancestors — just a fighter, perfect, for twenty seconds.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | 20 s |
| Effects | Time perception ×0.75 (world slows for the player); auto-slip: the first incoming punch every 3 s is slipped automatically; stamina frozen (no drain); counter windows +200 ms; punches +15% speed |
| Presentation | Desaturated world, breath audio forward, single-color aura seep (fighter's element), crowd audio drops to heartbeat. The *quietest* transformation — intentionally. Arc 1's climax is mastery, not magic |
| Comedown | Standard + stamina resumes at current value |

- **Unlock:** story (Arc 1 semifinal) + fighter level 20. Universal — every fighter can Zone; it's the democratic tier.

## 4. TIER 3 — SPIRIT MANIFESTATION (Arc 2)

The Keshin analog: the fighter's inner figure erupts behind them.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | 30 s |
| Manifest | An authored figure per named character (an ancestor in old hand-wraps, a colossal bear, a freight train of storm, the northern lights given shoulders). Manifests are *characterization* — content review requires each to say something true about its fighter |
| Effects | All damage ×1.4; hissatsu costs paid from a private 50-pt manifest meter instead of team Tension (the manifest brings its own fuel); manifest punches echo the fighter's punches at 0.3 s delay for +30% follow-up damage (the two-hit rhythm is the tier's feel signature); zone displacement effects doubled |
| **Manifest Clash** | If both active fighters manifest simultaneously: 5 s scripted clash sub-duel — rapid alternating counter-window exchanges at ×0.5 time; clash winner's manifest suppresses the loser's (ends it early). Clash resolution is pure timing skill, zero stats — the anime double-spread page, playable |
| Presentation | Full camera orbit break (the one place neutral framing bends): 2.5 s reveal script, arena reacts (frost creep / wind-torn banners per element), commentary has authored lines per named manifest |

- **Unlock:** Arc 2 story per-character events (a manifest must be *earned in fiction* — each named character's manifest debut is an episode beat). Generic stablemates: level 35 + a Zone mastery flag, receiving element-generic manifests.

## 5. TIER 4 — INHERITANCE (Arc 2 finale)

Mid-match, take on a stablemate's — or a departed mentor's — house style. The tear-jerker as a mechanic.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | 25 s |
| Requirement | Sworn bond (Lv5) with the source ([[NP_08_STABLE]] §5.2), OR a story-flagged legacy source (the departed coach — no bond level can be grinded for the dead; legacy Inheritances are authored gifts) |
| Effects | Caster temporarily gains: the source's house trait, their native technique set (cast from the private 50-pt meter), their tell dialect (confusing enemy PI reads — enemies literally misread you), and their stance/animation set. Own stats retained |
| Presentation | The source (if living and dressed) stands at the apron and *mirrors the stance*; ghost-frame of the source overlays the caster's silhouette. If legacy: the overlay is older film-grain footage. Yes, this will wreck people. That's the job |
| Comedown | Standard + the caster's own dialect scrambled 10 s (identity whiplash — flavor-priced) |

- Dual-house characters ([[NP_07_HOUSES]] §5) casting Inheritance of their *own second house*: cost 75 (it was always theirs).

## 6. TIER 5 — AWAKENING (Arc 3)

Latent lineage fully realized; the dual-house hybrid state; the protagonist's sealed house opens here.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | 40 s |
| Requirement | Story-gated per character: house SECRET learned + a personal Arc 3 trial. The protagonist's Awakening additionally requires the sealed-house resolution ([[NP_07_HOUSES]] §7) |
| Effects | Stacks a chosen prior tier's effect at 75% strength (Zone-Awakening, Manifest-Awakening, Inheritance-Awakening — build expression at the summit); +1 hybrid technique slot active; all three affinity rings' caps raised from ×1.5 to ×1.75 for the duration ([[NP_06_AFFINITY]] §4 exception, the only one); DROP scored while Awakened: +15 Tension refund (the comeback cascade — an Awakened drop can refuel the *next* fighter's transform) |
| Presentation | Full element-storm arena takeover; the fighter's house crest manifests in the environment; unique leitmotif per named character layered over the club theme. The loudest the game ever gets |

- Design guarantee (per [[NP_07_HOUSES]] §7): the protagonist's Awakening *adds to* the no-name build — nothing the player grew is invalidated at the summit.

## 7. COUNTERPLAY & THE WEATHERING OPTION

Transforms answer transforms — but the game must honor the player who saved nothing:

- **Weathering:** against any active transform, perfect-defense actions (perfect blocks, slips, clash wins) reduce incoming transform damage 50% and shave 1 s off its duration each. A flawless defensive 20 s can *starve* a Zone entirely. Weathering a boss transform without answering is an authored achievement ("Stood in the Storm") and commentary treats it as legendary.
- Comedown windows are the honest answer slot: 10 s, telegraphed, fair.

## 8. ECONOMY INTERACTION SUMMARY

The whole game funnels here: clean play → Tension ([[NP_05_TENSION_HISSATSU]]) → relay chains ([[NP_05_TENSION_HISSATSU]] §6) → the bar → the bet. Because the bar is shared and the cost is total, every transformation is a *team decision* the coach can influence (corner-phase "save it"/"spend it" calls are a staff active, [[NP_10_TACTICS]] §6) — the shonen ultimate as a resource-management argument between friends, which is exactly what it is in the source material.
