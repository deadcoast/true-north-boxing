## SCENE 2 — THE SAME GLOVE, FIFTY YEARS DOWN

**INT. THE OLD GYM — PRESENT — 7:00 AM**

Same building. The camera holds the same angle the broadcast used. Empty benches. One bulb in six working. RORY, mid-shadowbox, breath visible indoors.

**[PLAY]** Control transfers mid-combination — same verbs, stats now 25–30. One objective: finish the shadow round (Shadow Rounds minigame intro, [[NP_11_PROGRESSION]] §7, no ghost yet).

The door. COACH WHITLOCK, sixty, parka over a suit jacket, holding an envelope already opened. He watches one combination all the way through before he says anything.

**WHITLOCK:** Your left drops when you get tired.
**RORY:** It's seven in the morning.
**WHITLOCK:** Punches happen then too.

He puts the letter on the ring apron. Doesn't hand it over — puts it down. Rory reads it where it lies.

**INSERT — THE LETTER.** CRBF letterhead, bilingual. **[FR]** — the French column is the legally binding one and the framing device: the camera reads the French side first, then finds the English. Key clause highlighted by a coffee ring, decades of them:

> *"...failing demonstration of a competitive club — minimum two (2) segment victories in a sanctioned dual before season's end — the Millbrook charter, oldest in the federation, shall be dissolved and its history transferred to the receiving institution."*

**RORY:** Receiving institution.
**WHITLOCK:** Ascendant. Toronto. They collect charters like ours the way you'd collect stamps. *(beat)* They've requested the dual for Saturday. Their B-squad. They mean it as paperwork.
**RORY:** Sanctioned dual needs four dressed.
**WHITLOCK:** It does.
**RORY:** We have one.
**WHITLOCK:** You have five days.

He picks the letter back up, folds it, pins it to the gym wall — the first object on the board that becomes the Gym Wall ([[NP_08_STABLE]] §7). The camera marks the spot.

**[PLAY]** Overworld tutorial: movement, inspect, the club-room hub objects (stable locker — empty; Scout Book shelf — empty; the wall — one letter). Quest log entry, written in Whitlock's hand: *"Four dressed by Saturday. Start with someone smarter than us."*

**INT. LIBRARY — CONTINUOUS**

PRIYA SANDHU has a study carrel fortified like a corner office: three open binders, a tablet paused on fight tape, a printed bracket annotated in two colors of ink. Rory arrives mid-sentence — hers.

**PRIYA:** — because their R2 leads with her right on the third exchange. Every time. It's on tape four times and nobody in that building has noticed. *(looks up)* You're the boxing club.
**RORY:** You know the boxing club?
**PRIYA:** I know everything about the boxing club. Charter 1961 — recognized retroactive to '58, it's a whole thing. Four provincial titles, one national, February '76. *(taps the tablet)* I've watched the '76 dual eleven times. The tape's damaged. The R5 fighter — there's no name on the card, no name on the call, nothing in the CRBF digest. You'd think someone would have written it down. **[FR]** *(FR-CA authoring note: Priya's info-dumps run on Québécois broadcast idiom she's absorbed from RDS fight coverage — her French lines quote commentary phrasings from memory)*

**PRIYA:** You fold Saturday *anyway*. Ascendant B runs five dressed, all seeded, coached off a tablet. You have a captain with dropping-left-syndrome and an empty locker room. *(beat)* Two segment wins though. That's the clause? Two of three?

**PRIYA:** I read the *regulation the letter cites.* The letter paraphrases it badly. *(already packing the binders)* R1 is unwinnable for us, so it's a bank. R2 is winnable on points if — *(stops herself, looks at her own hands)* — if the person who's only ever watched can actually do any of it.
**RORY:** One way to find out.
